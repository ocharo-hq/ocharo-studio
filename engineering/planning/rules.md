# Engineering rules

*Last updated: 2026-09-25*

- Keep fixed-size asset selection and texture placement as the initial scope.
- Every garment declares its own parts, built-in attachments, and default material behaviour.
- Keep generated images distinct from source photography.
- Verify actual 3D behaviour before reporting the studio complete.
- Record tests, runtime evidence, and remaining limitations separately.
- The developer owns the first commit; later local commits require the native grant and the user's standing authorization.
- Publishing remains developer-owned.
